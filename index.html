Before you install.

Do I have to type in my funds?
No. Import the consolidated account statement (CAS) you download from CAMS, KFintech or MFCentral and every fund, folio and transaction comes in at once. Shares come from your broker’s holdings sheet and NPS from the CRA statement.
Does it need my bank or broker login?
Never. It reads files you already have. It cannot place orders, move money or see your accounts.
What happens after 14 days?
Nothing disappears. The core tracker — imports, holdings with XIRR, every asset and loan, your net-worth number, the guide, the calculators, backups — stays free for good. Planning, tax reports, the checks, family and exporting your own reports stay with a one-time purchase. There is no subscription.
Can I use it on my phone and my PC?
Yes. The Android app and the Windows program read the same backup file. Save on one and open on the other, or connect your own Google Drive and both devices share one backup folder.
Is it investment or tax advice?
No. It shows facts and estimates worked out from your own data, with the assumptions on screen. Check big decisions with a professional.
Do I pay once for both phone and PC?
The Android app and the Windows program are bought separately — one through Google Play, one through the Microsoft Store. Buy the one you use, or both; your data moves freely between them either way.
Are updates free?
Yes. Every update within the version you bought is free. If a major new version is ever released, existing users get it at a large discount.
