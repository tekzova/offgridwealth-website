Your first ten minutes.

The first time you open it, the app shows a short welcome tour and a set-up page. You can explore with a sample family before touching your own files.

  1. Import your statement

    Drop in a CAS, CAMS or MFCentral file. Funds, folios and every transaction come in at once.

  2. Add what else you own

    Shares, deposits, EPF, PPF, NPS, gold, property and loans — once, with the fields that matter.

  3. Build your plan

    Answer a planner’s questions and get a monthly amount, a mix and your goals.

Getting your statement

The best start is a consolidated account statement (CAS) covering CAMS and KFintech, with the full transaction history. Ask for the detailed version, from your first investment to today, on the CAMS, KFintech or MFCentral website. It arrives by email, usually as a password-protected PDF — OffGridWealth opens those.

Filing this year? Also download the MFCentral capital-gains report for that financial year.

Not ready? Tap Try with sample data. Two made-up families load in — Arjun and Meera, who follow a model plan, and Rajesh and Lakshmi, who make the mistakes the app is built to catch. They never count in your own totals.
