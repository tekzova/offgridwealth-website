Learn in nine seconds

Tap any word you don’t know.

Every underlined term in the guide opens its glossary entry: plain words, a rupee example, and what to do about it.

A book on Indian investing, inside the app.

Forty-nine chapters on what an investor in India — resident or NRI — should know before and while investing: how each investment works, what it costs, how it is taxed, what can go wrong, and how to keep a portfolio balanced over the years.

  • Learn — how everything works, chapter by chapter
  • Do — step-by-step workbooks
  • Plan — answer a few questions and get your own plan
  • Search the whole guide; it remembers what you have read
  • Black-on-white book layout, or your app theme
  • Nothing to import first — read it before you add a single number

Each chapter starts with the short version.

Every chapter opens with “In a nutshell” — the whole idea in three sentences — then goes deeper for those who want it. Rules and rates are dated, so you know how current they are.

  • Diagrams and worked examples in rupees
  • Links between chapters, with a Back button to where you were reading

173 terms, explained for a beginner.

XIRR, NAV, exit load, indexation, LTCG, sum assured, surrender value. Every term has three parts: what it means in simple words, an example with real rupee amounts, and what to do about it. Tap any underlined word in the guide to open it.

Not sure what a number means? Every screen in the app has a small ⓘ that explains it: what it does, what you give it and what you get.
